Local application · private use

A personal assistant with sources you can inspect.

Chris AI Personal Assistant helps its owner review information, consider priorities, and keep useful context. Authorized sources are collected into a local index; suggestions link back to supporting material.

This website is an information site. It does not host the assistant, offer public account registration, connect visitors to Google, or receive the owner's personal records. The assistant runs separately on the owner's computer.

Information and priorities

Review relevant messages and source material alongside the owner's stated goals. Distinguish confirmed facts, source statements, and uncertain suggestions.

Learning and discovery

Use authorized interests and public material to surface potentially useful information. A subscription or liked video is a limited signal, not proof that content has been watched or mastered.

Owner-controlled delivery

Read the local dashboard or receive selected summaries through the owner's configured Telegram bot. The bot is bound to the owner; it is not a public messaging service.

Visible evidence and controls

Inspect source records, correct suggestions, pause a source, and delete local records. AI output remains advice or a draft for the owner to review.

Optional Google connections

The OAuth application names Chris AI Gmail and Chris AI YouTube are two separate, read-only connections used by this same local personal assistant. Each requires the account owner's explicit Google authorization.

ConnectionWhat it readsPurpose

YouTube
youtube.readonly		The authorized account's channel identity, subscriptions, liked videos, and owner-selected playlists, as made available by the API.Local organization and context for information recommendations. This API connection does not provide watch history.

These connectors do not send or modify email, change subscriptions, add likes, post comments, or edit playlists. Data is accessed through Google's APIs rather than by collecting Google passwords.

Local storage, with disclosed external processing

Source records, evidence, and the search index are stored on the owner's computer. When AI analysis is enabled, bounded, relevant excerpts may be sent to the configured AI service. The current implementation supports Codex through the owner's ChatGPT subscription and an optional, explicitly enabled DeepSeek service. Selected output may be sent through Telegram.

Local storage does not mean every processing step stays offline. Read the privacy notice for data categories, recipients, retention, and deletion limits.
